### 4. Exact Slide Copy to Paste
- **Slide Title:** **FLOW OF SOLUTION**
- **Step 1: Real-Time Urban Data Ingestion**
  - Continuous synchronization with OpenStreetMap road networks.
  - Live API integration with **Gurugram GMDA OneMap GIS** & **CCMS Streetlight Automation Panels** for electrical outage status.
  - Verified POI database tracking late-night businesses & Safe Haven check-ins.
- **Step 2: Geospatial & Temporal Scoring Core**
  - Road network segmented into discrete 50-meter PostGIS geometry entities.
  - Valhalla C++ routing engine applies dynamic pedestrian cost penalizing dark, isolated, or unpaved segments.
  - Multi-objective Pareto optimization produces 3 distinct candidates: *Safest Route*, *Balanced Route*, *Fastest Route*.
- **Step 3: User Journey & Client-Side Guardianship**
  - User selects route; Journey Guardian state machine initiates.
  - On-device TFLite audio classifier listens for human distress screams (16kHz mono, runs locally without uploading audio).
  - Safety Hold thumb sensor monitors user alertness while crossing high-risk zones.
- **Step 4: Threat Escalation & Safe Haven Dispatch**
  - **Level 1 (Subtle):** Fake incoming call trigger to deter suspicious persons.
  - **Level 2 (Duress):** Secret Duress PIN confirms threat while displaying false cancel screen.
  - **Level 3 (SOS):** Automatic deep-link to 112 emergency dialer, Twilio SMS broadcast with live tracking link, and offline 128-byte BLE mesh broadcast.

### 5. Speaker Notes (45 Seconds)
> *"Here is how the data flows end-to-end. On the backend, we ingest road geometries from OpenStreetMap and real-time streetlight fault data directly from Gurugram GMDA’s CCMS panels. Our PostGIS engine splits every road into 50-meter segments and passes them to Valhalla with custom pedestrian costing algorithms that penalize unlit alleys and reward sidewalks and open stores. On the frontend, our React Native app runs an on-device TensorFlow Lite model that detects distress sounds locally without sending audio to the cloud. If danger occurs, our system triggers a multi-channel response: it connects to 112, alerts trusted contacts, broadcasts via offline Bluetooth mesh, and points the user to the nearest open Safe Haven 2 minutes away."*
